About the photographer

My name is
Muegbeyogho Courage Nosakhare,
I’m a Photographer.

I create portraits, event and editorial photographs that help you feel at ease in front of the camera and give you images you’ll be proud to keep and share.

As a photographer, I look for a considered frame that needs minimal post-production. As a retoucher, I refine each image without leaving traces; as an educator, I help students build a process they can repeat.

From personal portraits to events and editorial work, I listen closely, keep the process relaxed, and focus on photographs that feel like you.
